There is no shortage of people who would take a Crystal job. There is a shortage of places to tell them the job exists.

CrystalGigs is that place, and it does one thing. You post a role, it goes on the board, and developers reading the other three sites see it.

A few details that matter more than they sound.

Every live posting carries structured data, so a search engine can index it as a job rather than as a page that happens to mention one. That is the difference between a posting people find and a posting people stumble onto.

An expired posting stops advertising itself. When a role's window closes the page reports that it is gone instead of sitting there looking open, because a board full of filled roles is worse than a small board.

Nothing here is scraped. Every listing was posted by whoever is doing the hiring.

If you are hiring Crystal developers, post it. If you are not hiring but you know someone who is, send them here.
