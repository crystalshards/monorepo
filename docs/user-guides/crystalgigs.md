## Introduction

### What is CrystalGigs.org?

CrystalGigs.org is the official job board for the Crystal programming language community. It connects Crystal developers with companies looking to hire Crystal talent.

Unlike generic job boards, CrystalGigs focuses exclusively on Crystal-related positions, ensuring:
- Higher quality matches
- Less noise and spam
- Direct connection to Crystal community
- Relevant opportunities for Crystal developers

### For Job Seekers

**Benefits:**
- Curated Crystal development opportunities
- Remote-friendly listings
- Salary transparency
- Direct company contact
- No recruiter spam
- Free for job seekers

**Job Types:**
- Full-time positions
- Part-time roles
- Contract work
- Remote opportunities
- Freelance projects

### For Employers

**Why Post on CrystalGigs:**
- Reach engaged Crystal developers
- Quality over quantity
- Active community members
- Direct applications

- Affordable pricing

**Pricing:**

### Why Post on CrystalGigs?

### Job Posting Guidelines

**What to Include:**

1. **Clear Job Title**
   - Be specific: "Senior Backend Engineer (Crystal)" not "Developer"
   - Include level: Senior, Mid-level, Junior
   - Avoid jargon: "Ninja", "Rock Star", "Guru"

2. **Detailed Description**
   - What you're building
   - The role's impact
   - Day-to-day responsibilities
   - Team structure and size
   - Growth opportunities

3. **Technical Requirements**
   - Required Crystal experience
   - Other language requirements
   - Framework knowledge
   - System design skills
   - DevOps experience

4. **Company Information**
   - What your company does
   - Company size and stage
   - Culture and values
   - Remote work policy
   - Office locations

5. **Compensation**
   - Salary range (required)
   - Equity/stock options
   - Benefits package
   - Bonuses
   - Professional development budget

6. **Application Process**
   - How to apply
   - What to include
   - Timeline expectations
   - Interview process overview

**What NOT to Include:**

- ❌ Unrealistic requirements
- ❌ Unpaid work/"exposure"
- ❌ Vague job descriptions
- ❌ Hidden salaries
- ❌ "Competitive salary" without range
- ❌ Discriminatory language
- ❌ Multi-level marketing schemes

### Creating a Job Listing

**Step-by-Step Process:**

1. **Visit CrystalGigs.org**
   - Click "Post a Job" button
   - Review pricing options

2. **Fill Out Job Form**
   ```
   Required Fields:
   ✓ Job Title
   ✓ Company Name
   ✓ Job Type (Full-time, Part-time, Contract, Remote)
   ✓ Location
   ✓ Salary Range
   ✓ Job Description (min 100 words)
   ✓ How to Apply
   ✓ Contact Email

   Optional Fields:
   ○ Company URL
   ○ Company Logo
   ○ Application URL
   ○ Tags/Categories
   ```

3. **Preview Listing**
   - Review how your job appears
   - Check for typos
   - Verify links work
   - Ensure formatting is correct

4. **Choose Listing Type**
   - Standard ($99/30 days)
   - Featured ($299/30 days)

5. **Proceed to Payment**
   - Stripe checkout
   - Credit card or other payment methods
   - Instant activation after payment

6. **Job Goes Live**
   - Immediately visible after payment
   - Confirmation email sent
   - Edit link provided

### Managing Your Listings

**After Posting:**

You'll receive an email with:
- Link to your live job posting
- Admin link to edit or deactivate
- Application tracking (if using CrystalGigs applications)
- Performance statistics

**Editing Active Jobs:**

1. Use edit link from confirmation email
2. Make changes to job details
3. Save and republish
4. Changes appear immediately

**Deactivating Early:**

If you fill the position:
1. Use admin link
2. Click "Mark as Filled"
3. Job removed from active listings
4. No refunds for early deactivation

**Extending Listings:**

To extend beyond 30 days:
1. Original listing expires
2. Create new listing
3. Or contact support@crystalgigs.org for bulk discounts

## Job Posting Requirements

### Required Information

**Job Title**
- Clear and specific
- Include seniority level
- Mention Crystal if not obvious

**Company Name**
- Legal company name or DBA
- No anonymous postings
- Build trust with candidates

**Job Type**
- Full-time
- Part-time
- Contract
- Remote
- Or combination

**Location**
- City and Country
- Or "Remote" if fully remote
- "Remote OK" if flexible
- Be honest about requirements

**Salary Range**
- Minimum and maximum
- Currency (USD, EUR, etc.)
- Include equity/bonuses if significant
- Transparency required

**Job Description**
- Minimum 100 words
- Clear responsibilities
- Required qualifications
- Nice-to-have skills
- Company overview

**Application Method**
- Email address, or
- Application URL
- Response time expectations

### Description Guidelines

**Structure:**

```markdown
# About [Company Name]

Brief company overview (2-3 sentences)

# The Role

What you'll be doing day-to-day

# Responsibilities

- Build and maintain Crystal services
- Design scalable APIs
- Collaborate with frontend team
- Participate in code reviews
- Mentor junior developers

# Requirements

- 3+ years backend development
- Strong Crystal experience
- PostgreSQL expertise
- API design skills
- Git proficiency

# Nice to Have

- Open source contributions
- Crystal shard authorship
- DevOps experience
- Distributed systems knowledge

# Our Tech Stack

- Crystal (backend services)
- Lucky framework
- PostgreSQL + Redis
- Docker + Kubernetes
- GitHub Actions

# What We Offer

- Competitive salary: $140k-180k
- Health insurance
- 401(k) matching
- Remote-first culture
- Learning budget
- Conference attendance

# How to Apply

Send resume and GitHub profile to: hiring@company.com
```

### Content Quality

**Dos:**
- ✅ Be specific about responsibilities

- ✅ Transparent about salary
- ✅ Describe company culture
- ✅ Explain growth opportunities
- ✅ Include technical stack details
- ✅ Clear application instructions

**Don'ts:**
- ❌ Copy-paste generic descriptions
- ❌ Laundry list of every technology
- ❌ Unrealistic requirements
- ❌ Vague responsibilities

## Best Practices

### Writing Effective Job Posts

**1. Start Strong**
```
❌ Bad: "We're looking for a developer"
✅ Good: "We're building a real-time analytics platform
         in Crystal and need a senior backend engineer
         to lead API development"
```

**2. Be Specific**
```
❌ Bad: "5-10 years experience"
✅ Good: "3+ years building production APIs, with
         at least 1 year of Crystal experience"
```

**3. Sell the Role**
```
❌ Bad: "Write code, fix bugs"
✅ Good: "Design and implement high-performance
         microservices handling 10M+ requests/day.
         Your work directly impacts 1M+ users."
```

**4. Highlight Growth**
```
❌ Bad: "Room for advancement"
✅ Good: "Clear path to Staff Engineer within 2 years.
         Annual conference budget. Weekly tech talks.
         Dedicated mentor for first 3 months."
```

### Attracting Qualified Candidates

**Salary Transparency:**
```
❌ Avoid: "Competitive salary"
✅ Better: "$120,000 - $160,000 + equity"
```

**Remote Work:**
```
❌ Vague: "Flexible work arrangements"
✅ Clear: "Fully remote, US timezones preferred.
          Team meets quarterly in person."
```

**Technical Details:**
```
❌ Generic: "Backend development"
✅ Specific: "Building gRPC services in Crystal.
             Optimizing PostgreSQL queries.
             Designing distributed systems."
```

### Salary Ranges

**Why Include Salary:**
- Saves time for both parties
- Shows respect for candidates
- Increases application rates
- Required by law in some regions

**Setting Ranges:**
- Research market rates
- Consider experience levels
- Factor in location (if relevant)
- Include equity if significant
- Be realistic

**Example Ranges:**
```
Junior Developer:     $70k - $90k
Mid-level Developer:  $90k - $130k
Senior Developer:     $130k - $180k
Staff/Principal:      $180k - $250k+
```

Adjust for:
- Location (SF/NYC higher)
- Company size
- Funding stage
- Equity compensation

### Remote Work Policies

**Be Crystal Clear:**

```
✅ Fully Remote
   "Work from anywhere. No office required."

✅ Remote-First
   "Remote by default. Optional office in SF."

✅ Hybrid
   "3 days in office (NYC), 2 days remote."

✅ Remote-Friendly
   "Office in Austin. Remote for right candidate."

❌ Flexible Arrangement
   (Too vague - specify details)
```

### Response Time

**Set Expectations:**

```
"We review applications within 3 business days.
First-round interviews scheduled within 1 week.
Typical hiring process: 2-3 weeks total."
```

**Stay Responsive:**
- Acknowledge applications quickly
- Update candidates on status
- Don't ghost applicants
- Provide feedback when possible

## FAQ & Support

### For Job Seekers

**Q: Is CrystalGigs free for job seekers?**
A: Yes! Job seekers never pay to apply or browse listings.

**Q: Do I need an account to apply?**
A: No. Most applications go directly to the employer's system.

**Q: How do I report a suspicious job posting?**
A: Email abuse@crystalgigs.org with the job URL and details.

**Q: Can I post my resume for employers to find?**
A: Not currently. Feature under consideration for future release.

**Q: How do I unsubscribe from job alerts?**
A: Click "Unsubscribe" link in any job alert email.

**Q: Are international jobs posted here?**
A: Yes! Jobs from around the world, including remote positions.

### For Employers

**Q: How long does my job posting stay active?**
A: 30 days from payment. You can deactivate early if position is filled.

**Q: Can I edit my job after posting?**
A: Yes! Use the edit link in your confirmation email.

**Q: Do you offer bulk posting discounts?**
A: Yes! Contact sales@crystalgigs.org for 5+ jobs.

**Q: Can I get a refund if I don't find candidates?**
A: Refunds available within 24 hours only. We can't guarantee candidate quality.

**Q: How do I make my job featured?**
A: Select "Featured" option during checkout. Can upgrade existing posts.

**Q: Do you support recurring job posts?**
A: Not automated. Repost when previous expires or contact for enterprise solutions.

**Q: Can I screen candidates before they contact me?**
A: Currently we provide direct links. Application screening features planned.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: Credit/debit cards via Stripe. NET30 invoicing for enterprise (5+ jobs).

### General Questions

**Q: Why do job postings cost money?**
A: Paid postings reduce spam and ensure serious employers, creating better experience for job seekers.

**Q: How is CrystalGigs different from other job boards?**
A: We focus exclusively on Crystal, creating a targeted community for Crystal developers and employers.

**Q: Who runs CrystalGigs?**
A: Part of the CrystalShards ecosystem, run by Crystal community members.

**Q: How do you prevent spam and scams?**
A: Paid postings, manual review, community reporting, and quick removal of problematic listings.

**Q: Can I share job postings on social media?**
A: Yes! Every job has a unique URL. Share freely.
